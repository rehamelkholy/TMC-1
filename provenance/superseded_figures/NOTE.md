# Superseded Figure 3 files

`fig3.pdf`, `fig3.png` and `fig3_data.csv` here are the Figure 3 files produced before amendment 9 (R2). In them the `high_logcd` and `low_dist` fixed-ordering baseline curves averaged 200 random tie-breaks. The current files in `figures/` use the exact tie expectation (amendment 9, R2) and were regenerated with `scripts/52_fig3_feedback.py`. 30 of 805 rows of `fig3_data.csv` differ, all in those two baseline curves; the feedback-learner curve and the consistency check are unchanged. The frozen files are kept here unmodified.
