AMENDMENTS to protocol_v1 (2026-09-21; recorded before any evaluation; protocol_v1.md is unchanged)
A1 Inclusion: a detection counts only if (i) toward TMC-1, (ii) non-isotopologue, (iii) first FIRM detection
   (tentative reports do not count), (iv) SMILES encodes the connectivity, hydrogen placement and net
   charge stated in the discovery paper; bond-order/spin representation is documented, not matched on.
A2 Dates: first-public-date = earliest arXiv v1 date; else journal accepted date; else online date. Record
   the proxy used.
A3 Known-at-origin: any detection dated before an origin (including pre-2021-08 ones absent from Lee's
   anchors, e.g. C3N-, C5N-) is known at that origin: removed from the test pool, never a test positive.
A4 Sensitivity: every headline result is also reported (a) with low/unresolved-date in-list detections
   removed and (b) with any detection within 60 days of an origin moved to the other side.
A5 Labels: rule A/B/C from protocol_v1, implemented with the F3 InChI-skeleton keys.
