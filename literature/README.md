# Literature screening

* `ads_queries.txt` - the ADS queries used for the two literature searches (2013-2021 and 2021-2026), verbatim.
* `ads_screening_2021_2026_ids.csv` (371 records) and `ads_screening_2013_2021_ids.csv` (169 records) - the screening outcome for every
  record returned: bibcode, DOI, arXiv id, title and the screening decision with its reason. The abstracts and the raw ADS exports are
  not redistributed (publisher text); the screening decisions were made on them. `record_id` is the key into the original exports.
* The `note`/`species_list`/`date_basis`/`first_public_date` columns carry the verification notes of the screening. These notes are
  reproduced as written when the records were screened, including references to review steps of that time.
